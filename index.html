Hi! My name is Ritesh Harshe. I am a PhD scholar at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (ICTS-TIFR), Bangalore. My principal advisor at ICTS is Prof. Ajith Parameswaran. I work in gravitational-wave data analysis. My current research focuses on various effects of gravitational-wave lensing in the gravitational-wave observations. I have contributed in the development of deep neural networks for the early-warning of binary neutron star mergers. I am also interested in the stochastic gravitational-wave background data analysis and applications thereof.
