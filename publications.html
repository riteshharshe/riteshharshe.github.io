Could the high-mass black holes in gravitational-wave observations be explained by lensing?
Published
Ritesh Harshe, R. Prasad, Parameswaran Ajith
We show that gravitational lensing cannot explain the high-mass black holes observed by the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA Collaboration. The observed events therefore imply the existence of intrinsically high-mass black holes in the Universe.
